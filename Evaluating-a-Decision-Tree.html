5.3 Strengths & Weaknesses

Know when to reach for a tree — and when not to.

Strengths

Easy to read and explain · handles non-linear boundaries · needs little data preparation · works with mixed data types.

Weaknesses

Prone to overfitting · a small data change can produce a very different tree · boundaries are blocky, not smooth.

5.4 From One Tree to a Forest

What if you grew hundreds of slightly different trees?

A Random Forest trains many trees on random slices of the data and features, then lets them vote. Each tree overfits a little differently — so their mistakes cancel out, and the group prediction is far more stable than any single tree.
